# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-12._

**Keep OTP collects no data.** There is no server, no account, no analytics,
no advertising, and no crash reporting. The released Android app declares no
network permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.

## What the app stores, and where

| Data | Where | Leaves the device? |
|---|---|---|
| OTP secret keys, account names, issuers | OS secure storage on your device | No |
| App-lock settings | OS secure storage on your device | No |
| Backup files you create | A location **you** choose | Only if you move or share the file yourself |

Android auto-backup is disabled for this app, so your data is not uploaded to
Google Drive or transferred by device migration. To move to a new phone, create
an encrypted backup and import it there.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — only to scan a QR code when you add an account. Images are
  processed on your device and are never stored or transmitted.
- **Biometrics / device credential** — only to unlock the app. The app never
  sees your fingerprint, face data, or PIN; the operating system performs the
  check and reports success or failure.

## Third parties

The QR scanner uses Google ML Kit's on-device barcode model. Network access is
removed from the app, so the library cannot report usage data.

## Your control

Everything is local, so you control it directly: remove an account in the app,
or uninstall the app to delete all stored data. Backup files you exported are
yours to delete.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from
anyone.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the change will be recorded in the release notes and
the date above updated.
